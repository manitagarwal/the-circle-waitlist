The Circle

A door that opens for very few.

The Circle is a private space we're building — quietly, for now — for people who are already, in their own way, doing exceptional things.

Inside, members gather around what they actually enjoy — a badminton court at 8, Sunday brunch, a night out that starts as three messages and becomes six people. You post the moments, you keep the people. We handle the friction of making plans happen.

The rest — how members are found, how the room is shaped — we're keeping to ourselves for now. What we can say: entry isn't automatic. It's earned, or you're vouched for by someone already in.

What members do inside

✦

Channels for the things you're into

Tennis, badminton, clubbing, brunch — join the rooms that match how you actually spend your time, or start a private one with your own circle of friends.

✦

Plans that fill themselves

Want badminton at 8 tonight? Book the slot. The moment three others do the same, we turn it into a real court booking — no group chat chasing required.

✦

A profile that's actually yours

Post the photos and videos from what you get up to, on the app and off it. Send and accept friend requests with the people you meet.

✦

Direct messages, and rooms of your own

Message one-to-one, or spin up a private channel for the friends you've made — yours to run, invite-only by default.
