# Marketing plan (first draft, Oct 2026)

Plain version. Assumptions are marked so you can correct them. Nothing here needs a big budget.

## 1. What we are selling
A private group of people aged about 21 to 28 in Delhi NCR who actually show up. You plan things together (padel, cafes, quizzes, treks, events), you can see how reliable people are, and you only get in by being picked.

The three things people should remember:
1. It is invite-only, so the people are chosen.
2. People show up (the reliability score is the reason).
3. It is easy: tap, hold to join, go.

## 2. Who we want first
- Working people and final-year students, 21 to 28, in Delhi, Gurgaon and Noida.
- Moved to the city or changed jobs, and has a thin friend circle.
- Already does things on weekends but plans through messy WhatsApp groups that fall apart.

Not for launch: tourists, teenagers, anyone outside NCR (we collect their email and tell them when we open).

## 3. The line
Keep the website line: "Most people won't get in. That's the point."

Supporting lines, all in the dry Delhi voice we already use in the app:
- "Plans that actually happen."
- "Say yes, then show up. Novel idea."
- "A group chat that does not die by Tuesday."

## 4. The one thing to get right: the waitlist
The queue number is our best asset. Someone who is #433 and tells friends has done our marketing.

What to do:
1. After applying, the person gets a shareable card: "I'm #433 for The Semi Circle." Easy to post to Stories and WhatsApp.
2. Each person has a referral code. When a friend applies with it, the friend is marked "vouched". Decision for you: do vouched people move up the queue, or only get looked at sooner? Moving up is stronger, but it weakens "chosen". I suggest "looked at sooner", never "skip the queue".
3. A simple "friends who applied with your code" count on their profile.
4. Close the loop: when they are let in, they get a message that makes them proud, and a way to vouch for two friends.

## 5. Where people come from
Ranked by what is cheap and likely to work for this crowd.

1. **Personal networks (week 1 to 6).** You, the team and the first members each bring 10 to 20 people they would actually vouch for. This sets the quality bar. Aim for the first 100 to 150 members through this alone.
2. **Instagram (ongoing).** A page that looks like the app: full photos, dry captions, no stock-ad feel. Reels of the real thing once meetups run.
3. **WhatsApp communities (week 3 on).** Join and sponsor existing running, padel, board-game, comedy and startup groups in NCR. Offer their members a few early invites rather than posting links.
4. **Offices and colleges.** Co-working spaces, startup communities, a few college societies. One short pitch and a few invites each.
5. **Venues as partners.** Padel courts, cafes, quiz pubs. They get a full room, we get a place and a mention. Ask for a banner or a QR at the venue.
6. **Small creators.** 5 to 10 local creators with 5k to 30k followers who fit. Give them invites, not money. Paid posts come later.

Do not do for now: paid ads, big influencers, PR, press release, cold DMs to strangers.

## 6. Content (about 3 posts a week)
- **The queue.** Posts with the live queue number. "433 waiting. 40 in."
- **The rules.** Short posts about how it works: reliability, hold to join, invite-only.
- **Real plans.** Photos and short clips from meetups. This is the main content once running.
- **Dry humour.** One-liners about Delhi plans (traffic, "I'm 5 minutes away", the group chat that dies).
- **Members.** First names, with permission: "Aarav, Gurgaon, 26. Joined for padel."

Voice rule: dry and a little teasing, never salesy, no exclamation marks, no emoji.

## 7. First 12 weeks
- **Weeks 1 to 2:** set up Instagram, build the share card and referral loop, write 15 posts ahead, pick 2 or 3 first events.
- **Weeks 3 to 6:** first batch of invites to personal networks. Run 2 events a week. Photograph everything.
- **Weeks 7 to 10:** open WhatsApp community partnerships and venue partners. First creator invites. Let in a second batch.
- **Weeks 11 to 12:** look at the numbers, keep what works, drop what does not.

## 8. Numbers to watch
- Applications per week and where they came from (referral code, source link).
- Share of applicants who share their queue card.
- Share of invited people who finish setup within 3 days.
- Share of members who join a booking in their first 7 days.
- Share who show up (reliability) in the first month.
- Referrals per member.

If people apply but do not join a booking, the problem is the product, not the marketing.

## 9. What we need to build (small)
1. Queue share card.
2. "Friends who applied with your code" on the profile.
3. Source tags on website links (for example `?src=insta`), saved with the application and shown in Admin.
4. A short "welcome and vouch two friends" message when someone is accepted.
5. A simple launch page for partner venues and communities.

## 10. Questions for you
1. Budget: is it close to zero, or a small monthly amount (say 10k to 30k rupees)?
2. Launch date: a rough month.
3. Who are the first 20 people you would vouch for, and in which circles?
4. The vouch decision in section 4: "looked at sooner" or "moves up the queue"?
5. Are you comfortable showing real member photos on Instagram, with permission?
