# Ad: "Everyone's on an app. No one's at the plan." (about 30 seconds, vertical 9:16)

Main character: a woman. Outside narrator. Two failed apps. The ending is the half-circle logo and "The Semi Circle", not a web address.

## How it is made
1. Generate 4 clips in Gemini (about 8 seconds each) from the prompts below. No text, logos or music in the clips.
2. Narration is made in Runway (voice: Arjun, or any other) from the lines below.
3. Text overlays, the logo end card, music and the final mix are added in post.

## Narration (outside narrator, dry, calm)
1. "Friday night. Her friends had plans. Just not with her."
2. "So she tried the apps. One had four hundred people. Nobody came."
3. "Another had three hundred. Nobody talked."
4. "Then she got an invitation."
5. "Small. Chosen. Everyone shows up."
6. "The Semi Circle. Most people won't get in. That's the point."

## On-screen text (added in post, our fonts)
- Over app one: "Swiped."
- Over app two: "Crowded."
- After the invitation: "Plans that happen."
- End card: the half-circle mark drawing itself, "The Semi Circle" beneath it, small line "By invitation only." No web address.

## Gemini prompts

### Paste this first (context and style, applies to every clip)
```
I am making a short vertical (9:16) ad for a new private community app in Delhi called The Semi Circle. It is for people aged 21 to 28 who want plans with friends that actually happen. Members are chosen, and the idea is that people show up. I will give you 4 short clips to generate one at a time. Keep these rules for ALL of them:

Main character: Ananya, a 26 year old Indian woman, shoulder-length black hair, warm brown skin, an olive-green jacket over a cream top, small silver hoop earrings. She must look the same in every clip.

Visual style: warm, cinematic, soft natural light, shallow depth of field, gentle handheld camera, muted warm tones with ivory and charcoal and touches of terracotta. Real Delhi and Gurgaon feel. Calm, a little dry and funny, never loud.

Strict rules: no text, no letters, no subtitles, no logos, no brand names and no watermarks anywhere in the frame. Any phone or app screens must be soft and unreadable. No music and no spoken dialogue, only natural ambient sound. No other brands. Vertical 9:16.

Reply "ready" and wait for the first clip.
```

### Clip 1: the ditch (8s)
```
Clip 1. Friday evening, golden hour turning to dusk, inside a small tidy Delhi flat. Ananya, dressed to go out, stands by the window holding her phone. Close shot of her face as her expression slowly drops while she reads messages (the screen is blurred and unreadable, a chat glowing softly). She exhales and sits on the edge of the sofa. Cut to a wide shot through the window: the city outside lit up, a busy lane, couples and groups walking past. She looks down at her shoes. Slow push-in, soft ambient room sound and distant traffic. Mood: quiet, a little funny and a little sad.
```

### Clip 2: the apps (8s, two quick scenes)
```
Clip 2. Two short scenes, with a hard cut between them.
Scene A (first 4 seconds): a small cafe table for two, set with two chai glasses. Ananya sits alone, checking her phone (blurred screen). A waiter calmly lifts the second chair away. Deadpan, dry humour, warm cafe light.
Scene B (last 4 seconds): a large noisy hall packed with about 300 strangers holding drinks, everyone talking to someone else. Ananya stands in the middle with a plain blank name tag stuck slightly crooked on her jacket, looking lost and politely smiling. Wide shot, then a slow push to her face. No readable text anywhere.
```

### Clip 3: the invitation (8s)
```
Clip 3. Close-up of Ananya's thumb pressing and holding a dark rounded button on a phone screen (the screen is soft and unreadable). A soft light fills the button from left to right as she holds it. Her face lights up with a small surprised smile. Then a smooth cut at the exact moment the button fills to a wide, golden dawn shot of a padel court in an Indian city, long soft shadows, mist, birds. Warm and hopeful.
```

### Clip 4: the circle (8s)
```
Clip 4. Dawn at a padel court. Ananya, in the same olive-green jacket, laughs in the middle of a rally with 5 friends (mixed, 22 to 28, natural and relaxed). Quick cuts: a ball in slow motion, high-fives, then the group sitting on a bench with steaming chai in small glasses, cheers, genuine laughter. End on a close shot of Ananya smiling at the group, soft golden light, camera slowly pulling back. Everyone is clearly there and happy. No text.
```

## Tips for Gemini
- Generate clips one at a time. If her face changes between clips, regenerate with a reference image (create the character picture first, then use it as a reference if your Gemini plan allows).
- If a clip shows text or logos, regenerate. We add all text ourselves.
- If a clip comes out longer or shorter than 8s, that is fine; I trim to the narration.
