# Brand decisions

## Tagline (final)
**Log in to log off.**

Why: the app exists to get people off the app and into real plans. It plays on the half circle (incomplete until people show up) and is a confident paradox in the same spirit as the best category taglines, without borrowing any of their words. Run a trademark check on the exact phrase before wide use.

## Positioning rules
- Do not name a city in branding or positioning (taglines, bios, deck cover, slogans). We operate in one city today, but the brand is not about one city. A city appears only where it is a fact (for example the application asks where you live, and the live-city logic in the app).
- Describe the product as a curated community for real-world plans: people apply and we pick. Do not say "invite only".
- Voice: dry, warm, confident. No emoji, no dashes, no exclamation marks.

## Descriptor (working, to confirm)
A community for plans that actually happen.
