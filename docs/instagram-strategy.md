# Instagram strategy (draft 1)

Constraints: near-zero budget, the account stays anonymous (no founder faces or names), content made with free tools (Canva, our own site and app, AI stills). Voice rule from the marketing plan: dry, a little teasing, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no dashes.

## 1. The one job of the account
Make a 23 year old in Delhi NCR think "I want to be on that list" and tap the link. Not followers for their own sake. The only numbers that matter: profile visits, link taps, applications with `?src=insta`.

## 2. The idea the whole account hangs on
"Most people won't get in. That's the point." The account behaves like the product: selective, calm, a bit smug, never begging. It never says "join us". It reports on a door and a queue.

Running character: the Door. The account speaks as the person who runs the door and keeps the list. Anonymous by design, so no face is needed.

## 3. Account setup (day 1)
- Name: The Semi Circle. Handle: whatever is free closest to thesemicircle.
- Bio (3 lines): "Delhi NCR. Ages 21 to 28. By invitation only." / "433 waiting. 40 in." (update weekly by hand) / link to the site.
- Profile photo: the half circle mark on ivory. Highlights: How it works, The queue, Rules, Plans.
- Link: `thesemicircle.in/?src=insta`. Use `?src=insta_reel`, `?src=insta_story` etc. so we can see what works.
- Post the first 9 posts before telling anyone, so the grid looks finished.

## 4. Five content pillars (the 60 post bank is built from these)
| Pillar | What it is | Share | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Queue | Live numbers, "who got in this week" (no names), waitlist maths | 20% | Single image, Story |
| The Rules | How reliability works, hold to join, why invite only | 20% | Carousel |
| Delhi humour | One-liners about plans in NCR: "5 minutes away", the group chat that dies, traffic | 30% | Single image, carousel |
| The Plans | Real photos and clips from meetups, once running; until then, the activity photo library | 20% | Photo, carousel |
| Members | First name, area, why they joined, with permission | 10% | Carousel |

Before real events exist, lean on Delhi humour and the Queue and Rules. Do not fake members or fake meetups.

## 5. Formats and cadence
- 4 feed posts a week: 2 carousels and 2 single images. No Reels for now (decision: images and carousels only).
- Stories daily, 2 minutes: queue number, a poll ("Delhi or Gurgaon for padel"), a quiz screenshot from the site.
- Best times to test first: 8 to 9 pm weekdays, Sunday midday.

## 6. Anonymity rules
- No founder or team names or faces anywhere, including in comments.
- The account replies as "the Door" (no first-person names).
- Business email only; no personal links in bio.
- No real member photos without written permission, and never a full face with a full name.
- AI imagery is labelled if Instagram asks; no fake people presented as members.

## 7. Growth with no money
1. Comments and DMs: the account joins NCR padel, running, board game, comedy and startup pages and leaves dry, useful comments. 10 a day. No links.
2. The queue card: every applicant can share "I'm #433" to their Story. This is the best channel. Make it frictionless.
3. Shareable posts: design every carousel so the last slide is a screenshot-worthy line.
4. Collabs: ask 5 to 10 small NCR pages (5k to 30k) to do a Collab post with 3 early invites as payment.
5. Quiz and games on the site: the score card is made for Stories; add "tag a friend who would fail".
6. Venues: tag them in event photos once we run plans; ask them to repost.
7. Reddit and WhatsApp communities are separate channels, not the Instagram job.

Not doing: paid ads, giveaways with follow gates, buying followers, engagement pods, cold DMs to strangers.

## 8. First 4 weeks
- Week 0: set up the account, create the first 12 posts, pick 3 recurring formats.
- Week 1: 9 posts live, Stories daily, 10 comments a day on NCR pages. Tell nobody in your personal circle yet except 5 people who will actually share.
- Week 2: start the queue series, Check which pillar gets the most saves and shares.
- Week 3: first collab ask to small pages; add the quiz score card as a Story.
- Week 4: review. Keep the top two pillars, drop the bottom one.

## 9. What to measure
Per week: reach, saves and shares per post (shares matter most), profile visits, link taps, applications with `?src=insta`, applicants who share their queue card. If saves are high and applications are low, the link or form is the problem.
