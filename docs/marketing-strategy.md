# Marketing strategy (full plan, draft 1)

Replaces the thinking in `marketing-plan.md` for execution. Budget stays near zero. The page stays anonymous. Voice: dry, warm, no emoji, no dashes, no exclamation marks.

## 1. Goal and stage
Pre-launch to the first 100 members in Delhi NCR, then 300.
- Goal for weeks 1 to 4: a finished-looking Instagram page, 500 followers who are actually in Delhi NCR, 150 applications.
- Goal for weeks 5 to 8: first 100 members, 10 to 20 real plans run, first creator collabs live.
- Goal for weeks 9 to 12: 300 members, a waitlist that grows without our push, 3 to 5 recurring partners (venues or communities).

What success looks like is not followers. It is applications, people who join a plan in week one, people who show up, and people who bring a friend.

## 2. Reality check on the first ideas
| Idea | Verdict | Do this instead |
|---|---|---|
| Post 40 to 50 posts at once | Do not dump them. Instagram treats a burst as spam and the posts compete with each other for reach. A full grid does matter for first impressions. | Seed 12 to 15 posts over the 7 to 10 days before any outreach, then 1 a day. A new visitor sees a full grid, and each post still gets its own life. |
| Follow 70 people an hour | At 70 an hour (about 1,700 a day) the account will very likely hit an action block or be restricted. Instagram does not publish limits, but new accounts are treated cautiously. Bots and follow-unfollow tools also break Instagram's terms. | Follow and engage by hand, about 30 to 60 a day in week 1, rising slowly. Only follow accounts worth following: Delhi NCR communities, event pages, cafes, padel clubs, running clubs, creators. Leave a real comment on a post, not just a follow. Never unfollow in bulk. |
| Ask micro influencers for free collabs | Works, if the offer is real. Free works when the thing offered has value. | Offer membership, early access, a hosted plan for their followers, or a feature as a host. See section 5. |

## 3. Timeline
**Week 0 (setup)**
- Profile photo, bio, link in bio with `?src=insta`, highlights (How it works, The queue, Rules, Plans).
- A business email for the page. No personal names or faces.
- Create 15 posts ahead (batches 1 to 4 already cover most of it).
- Google Search Console and Analytics are live. Check Admin > Website visits daily.

**Weeks 1 to 2 (seed and warm up)**
- Post the first 12 to 15 posts at 1 a day.
- Follow and comment by hand, 30 to 60 a day, from a list of 150 target accounts (section 4).
- Tell 10 personal contacts to apply and share their queue card. No broad announcement yet.

**Weeks 3 to 4 (first reach)**
- Continue 1 post a day, 1 Story a day (poll or question).
- Start creator outreach with 10 micro creators, 3 a day.
- Open 2 WhatsApp or community partnerships.

**Weeks 5 to 8 (proof)**
- Run 2 real plans a week and photograph everything with permission.
- First 2 or 3 collab posts go live.
- Open a second batch of invitations. Let in members in small batches and say so.

**Weeks 9 to 12 (compound)**
- Turn the best posts into a recurring series.
- Start one partnership with a venue or club that brings regulars.
- Review what worked and stop what did not.

## 4. Instagram: content and community
**Content mix (daily post, rotating)**
- Useful lists (weekend plans, hidden spots): saved and shared.
- Relatable friendship lines and advice column ("Dear Semi Circle"): saved and sent to friends.
- Delhi humour and "types of people": tagged.
- Real plans and photos, once events run.
- Occasional how-it-works and queue posts.
Rule: one in four posts mentions what we do. The last slide of every carousel is the apply message.

**Cadence:** 1 feed post a day for the first 30 days, then 4 to 5 a week. 1 Story a day (poll, question box, quiz). Add videos when they are good, not to hit a number.

**Hashtags:** see `marketing/instagram/hashtags.md`.

**Community engagement (the safe version of the follow plan)**
- Build a list of 150 accounts: Delhi NCR communities, run clubs, padel and sports groups, board game and quiz pages, cafe and food pages, comedy and music pages, college societies, coworking spaces.
- Daily: follow 30 to 60 from the list, leave 10 real comments, reply to every comment and DM on our own posts.
- Use the question sticker to collect friend problems for the advice column.
- Check follower quality weekly: are they in Delhi NCR and in the 21 to 28 range?

## 5. Micro creator collabs
**Who:** creators with 5k to 50k followers, living in Delhi NCR, posting about food, cafes, weekends, fitness, comedy, student or young professional life. Prefer high comment rates over follower count.

**Tiers**
1. 5k to 15k: easiest to say yes, give membership and a hosted plan.
2. 15k to 50k: offer a featured host role and an invite for 3 of their friends.
3. Above 50k: only after we have proof.

**What we can offer without cash**
- A guaranteed spot at a plan, and 3 invites for their friends.
- A host role: they run a plan for their followers.
- A feature on our page (Collab post, Story shout-out).
- Early access and a say in what we build.

**Process**
- Week 3: shortlist 40, contact 10, expect 2 to 3 yes.
- Use the Collab post feature (it shows on both profiles), plus a Story.
- Give each creator a referral code and a `?src=` link to measure applications.
- Keep a tracker: name, handle, followers, status, link, applications.

**DM template (short)**
> Hi, we are The Semi Circle, a small private community in Delhi NCR for people who make plans and actually show up. We love what you post about [specific thing]. We would like to offer you a spot, plus 3 invites for friends. If you like it, we would also love a collab post. Happy to share details here or by email at hello@thesemicircle.in.

**Compliance:** in India, if a creator gets a product or service in return, the post should be marked as a collaboration or an ad (ASCI guidelines). Ask creators to use the Instagram "paid partnership" label or a clear "collab" tag. Do not pay for fake reviews or ask for hidden promotions.

## 6. Other free channels
- **WhatsApp and Telegram communities:** join running, padel, board game, comedy and startup groups. Contribute first, then offer their members a few early invites. Do not post links cold.
- **Offices and colleges:** a short pitch to 5 coworking spaces and 5 college societies, with a few invites each.
- **Venues:** cafes, padel courts and quiz pubs. Offer a full room on a slow day in return for a banner or QR code.
- **Newsletters and city blogs:** 5 to 10 Delhi lifestyle newsletters. Pitch a story: "The invite only plan app for Delhi's lonely twenty-somethings" (adjust to the truth: apply and we pick).
- **Reddit and Quora:** answer questions about making friends in Delhi, with no link in the first answers. Later, link where it fits.
- **Our own members:** the strongest channel. See section 7.

## 7. Growth loops inside the product
- **Queue card:** every applicant gets a shareable "I'm #433" card. Make it frictionless.
- **Referral code:** each member and applicant has one. Referred applications are looked at sooner, not moved up.
- **Vouch step:** optional and clearly skippable.
- **Accept email:** includes a prompt to vouch for two friends.
- **After a plan:** ask for one friend to invite to the next.
- **Scarcity with honesty:** "we read every application" and "we pick" are true. Never fake a number.

These are working targets, not promises. We adjust after week 4.

## 9. Budget
Zero is workable. If there is a small amount, spend in this order:
1. Photo help for events (the best content).
2. A small boost on the single best-performing post.
3. A gift for creators (a coffee or a plan hosted for free).
Do not buy followers, run engagement pods or use automation tools.

## 10. Risks and rules
- **Account safety:** no bots, no follow limits abuse, no mass DMs. A banned page loses everything.
- **Anonymity:** page and creator DMs come from the business account and hello@thesemicircle.in. Real names only with a person's agreement.
- **Authenticity:** no fake members, fake plans or invented numbers.
- **Consent:** photos of members only with permission.
- **Tone:** tease plans, never people.
