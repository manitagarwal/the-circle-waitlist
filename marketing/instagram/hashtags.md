# Hashtag plan

Rules
- 6 to 8 per post. Instagram now ranks on the post's content, so hashtags only help it get filed under the right place. More than 10 looks spammy.
- Every post: 3 location tags + 2 or 3 topic tags + 1 or 2 community tags. Change the set between posts, never paste the identical block twice.
- Put them at the end of the caption, after a blank line. Not in the first line.
- No follow4follow, likeforlike, or other growth tags. They attract the wrong people and can limit reach.
- I can't see live tag popularity from here. Check each tag in the Instagram search box once: prefer tags with roughly 50k to 3 million posts. Under 5k is invisible, over 10 million is a crowd. Swap any that look dead.
