2. Why we use it

  • To review applications and keep the community to real, verified people (your work email and LinkedIn link are used for this).
  • To run the service: show your profile to other members, match you to channels, bookings and events, and send you messages and notifications.
  • To keep people safe: handle reports, apply warnings, pauses and removals, and calculate the reliability score from attendance.
  • To email you the codes and notices you need (sign-in codes, acceptance, account changes).
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.

3. Who can see what

  • Other members can see your name, username, photo or avatar, age, area, field of work, bio, interests, how many bookings you have hosted, and when you joined. They do not see your phone number, emails, date of birth, exact address, gender, or LinkedIn link.
  • The exact venue address of a booking is shown only to people who have joined it.
  • Our team can see application details and account records so that we can review applications and act on reports.
  • Messages in channels are visible to that channel's members. Direct messages are visible to the people in the conversation. Our team may read messages only when they are reported or when needed to keep the service safe or comply with law.

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as hashes. Data is encrypted in transit. Access to member data inside our team is limited to people who need it, and admin actions require signing in with an admin account. No system is perfectly secure; if there is a breach that affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

8. Children

The Semi Circle is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger, and we remove such accounts when we find them.
